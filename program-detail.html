Leadership Mastery Program

Build the confidence, clarity, and skills to lead teams and projects with impact. A practical, mentor-led program designed for emerging African leaders.

by Jane Wanjiku ★ 4.9 (320 ratings) 👥 1,240 enrolled 🗓 8 weeks

About this program

The Leadership Mastery Program is an 8-week, mentor-led journey that turns capable professionals into confident leaders. Through weekly live sessions, practical assignments, and one-on-one feedback from Jane Wanjiku, you'll develop the mindset and toolkit to lead people and drive results in any organisation.

Designed with the African workplace in mind, the program blends global leadership frameworks with real, local case studies from Nairobi to Lagos.

What you'll learn

Curriculum

8 modules · 24 lessons

Requirements

  • A laptop or smartphone with a stable internet connection.
  • 2-3 hours per week for sessions and assignments.
  • Willingness to participate actively and apply lessons at work.
  • No prior leadership experience required.
